### ​🖥️ 1. Core System & Desktop

​**The Foundation:** A modern, Wayland-native experience centered on the COSMIC ecosystem, now equipped with essential peripheral and clipboard infrastructure.

- ​**Desktop Environment:** COSMIC (Epoch) including native Cosmic Files, Edit, Terminal, and Settings.
- ​**Display Manager:** Cosmic Greeter.
- ​**Security & Secrets:** KeePassXC (Primary secret service provider; GNOME Keyring is excluded).
- ​**Audio & Networking:** PipeWire, NetworkManager.
- ​**Wayland Utilities:** wl-clipboard (Mandatory for snippet management and dev work).
- ​**Hardware Control:** Piper (Gaming mouse configuration) and OpenRGB (Lighting control).

### ​⌨️ 2. Terminal & CLI Tools

​**The "Modern Unix" Kit:** Fast, GPU-accelerated utilities and shell enhancements.

- ​**Terminal:** Ghostty.
- ​**Shell & Prompt:** Zsh, Starship (Prompt), Tmux (Multiplexer).
- ​**Navigation:** Yazi (File Manager), Zoxide (cd replacement), Fzf (Fuzzy Finder).
- ​**Search & View:** Ripgrep (rg), Eza (ls), Bat (cat), Delta (Syntax-highlighting diffs).
- ​**Nix Workflow:** nh (Nix Helper), nom (Nix Output Monitor).
- ​**System & Utilities:** Bottom (btm), Du, Tldr, Unzip, Curl, Wget.
- ​**Calculation:** qalc (The CLI backend for Qalculate!).

### ​🛠️ 3. Development Environment

​**The Polyglot Stack:** Focused on clean LSP integration, declarative environments, and external service testing.

- ​**Editors:** VSCodium, Neovim.
- ​**Version Control:** Git, GitHub CLI (gh).
- ​**API & Database:** Bruno (Git-friendly API testing) and Beekeeper Studio (Clean GUI for SQL/NoSQL databases).
- ​**Language Support (LSPs & Runtimes):**
    - ​**Nix:** nixd or nil, nixpkgs-fmt.
    - ​**Logic/Scripting:** Python, Go, Rust, C, Lua.
    - ​**Data & Web:** Markdown, CSS, TOML, YAML/YML, JSON.
- ​**Environment Management:** direnv, nix-direnv.

### ​🐋 4. Virtualization, Containers & Homelab

​**Infrastructure as Code:** A mix of daemonless containers, robust VM management, and network diagnostics.

- ​**Containers:** Podman, Podman-compose.
- ​**Orchestration & Systems:** Komodo (Deployment/Pipelines), Incus (System containers).
- ​**Virtual Machines:** QEMU/KVM, Virt-manager.
- ​**Compatibility:** Distrobox (for mutable FHS environments).
- ​**Network Diagnostics:** Wireshark and Nmap (For monitoring container/VM traffic).
- ​**Automation:** Ansible (For local playbook testing and deployment).

### ​🌐 5. Internet & Communication

​**The Hardened Web:** Privacy-first browsing, secure messaging, and P2P data transfer.

- ​**Browsers:** LibreWolf (Gecko), Brave (Chromium), Ladybird (WebKit/Independent).
- ​**Messaging:** Vesktop (Discord with Wayland/Vencord), Signal Desktop, Element (Matrix).
- ​**Proton Suite:** Proton Mail, Proton VPN, Proton Bridge.
- ​**Networking:** Tailscale (Mesh VPN).
- ​**P2P:** qBittorrent (For downloading Linux ISOs and open-source datasets).

### ​🎨 6. Media, Graphics & Production

​**The Comprehensive Studio:** Professional 2D, 3D, manufacturing, and A/V production.

- ​**2D Design:** Krita (Primary raster), Inkscape (Vector).
- ​**3D & Engineering:** Blender, FreeCAD, OpenSCAD (Code-based modeling).
- ​**Hardware Manufacturing:** OrcaSlicer or PrusaSlicer (For 3D printing) and KiCad (For PCB/Electronics design).
- ​**Video Production:** OBS Studio (Recording/Streaming) and Kdenlive (Open-source Non-Linear Video Editor).
- ​**Audio Production:** Tenacity (Clean, telemetry-free audio cutting/editing).

### ​📂 7. Productivity & Office

​**Knowledge Management:** Local-first tools for documents, notes, and quick utilities.

- ​**Office Suite:** LibreOffice (Paired with LanguageTool for system-wide grammar checking).
- ​**Second Brain:** Obsidian (Markdown-based).
- ​**Utilities:** Qalculate! (The ultimate desktop calculator GUI).
- ​**Reading:** Zathura (Minimal PDF viewer) and Foliate (Modern GTK eBook/EPUB reader).

### ​🎮 8. Gaming & Entertainment

​**The Linux Gaming Suite:** Native entertainment backed by heavy-duty performance infrastructure.

- ​**Launchers:** Steam (with Proton GE and Protontricks enabled), Heroic Games Launcher, Bottles.
- ​**Performance Infrastructure:** Feral GameMode (CPU/IO optimization), Gamescope (Wayland micro-compositor), and MangoHud (Performance overlay).
- ​**Media Playback:** MPV (Video), ncspot (Spotify TUI), Plex Desktop.
